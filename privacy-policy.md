# Privacy Policy for z-ytm-alpha

This Privacy Policy explains how z-ytm-alpha ("the Bot") handles user data. The Bot is an educational prototype and proof of concept designed to stream audio from public audiovisual links.

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
* **Temporary Session Data:** The Bot only processes data in-memory during an active session (such as the public video links provided via commands). 
* **No Database:** We do not use any external databases, logs, or persistent storage. All session data is completely wiped as soon as the Bot restarts or the session ends.
* **No Personal Information:** The Bot does not collect, store, or track Discord user IDs, usernames, server logs, or message history.

### 2. Third-Party Services
The Bot processes public links provided by users to extract audio bytes for streaming. We do not share any data with third parties, except for the automated requests made to public video platforms to retrieve the audio stream.

### 3. Contact
If you have any questions about this prototype, you can contact the developer at: https://github.com/zeigel/discord-ytm-bot/issues
